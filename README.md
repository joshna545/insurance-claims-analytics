## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw insurance claims data into actionable business insights by analyzing policy performance, claim behavior, and profitability metrics.

### Key Objectives

✅ Data cleaning and validation  
✅ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with Python  
✅ Business-focused SQL querying and KPI development  
✅ Interactive Power BI dashboard creation  
✅ Identification of profitability risks and operational issues

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 📉 Portfolio Profitability
- Overall **Loss Ratio: 169%** — Claims significantly exceed premiums collected
- All policy types are currently loss-making

### 🚩 Claim Rejection
- **43.5%** of all claims are rejected (very high)
- Highest rejection in Home and Life insurance

### 👥 Customer Risk Segmentation
- Premiums are nearly flat across age groups despite varying claim behavior
- 18–30 age group faces the highest rejection rate

### ⚠️ Portfolio Risk
- Travel insurance dominates (41% of policies) — high concentration risk
- ₹68L+ stuck in pending claims
